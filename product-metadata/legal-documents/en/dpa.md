# Data Processing Agreement

Effective date: the effective date shown for the published version.

This Data Processing Agreement (DPA) forms part of the contract between the customer identified in the order (Customer) and [PXA LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [LEGAL FORM], [STREET, POSTCODE, CITY, CANTON, SWITZERLAND] (PXA). It applies where PXA processes personal data in Customer Content on behalf of Customer.

## 1. Roles and law

Customer is the controller or a processor authorized by the relevant controller. PXA is Customer's processor or subprocessor. Each party complies with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where applicable, the GDPR, UK GDPR, and other mandatory data-protection law (Data Protection Law).

Customer determines the lawfulness, purposes, categories, accuracy, and instructions for Customer Content. PXA is independently responsible as controller for its own account, billing, security, Legal, and service-administration data described in the Privacy Notice.

## 2. Processing details

Subject matter: provision of PXA Generator, Designer, Migration, Importer, PDF Viewer, Spreadsheet, API, SDK-backed server operations, storage, support, and related contracted services.

Duration: the contract term plus the limited return, deletion, backup-expiry, and legal-hold period described below.

Nature and purpose: receive, host, organize, render, generate, convert, extract, analyze, migrate, import, export, transmit, secure, back up where contracted, troubleshoot, and delete Customer Content according to Customer's use of the service.

Data subjects may include Customer users, employees, contractors, applicants, clients, suppliers, patients, passengers, or any other persons represented in Customer Content.

Personal data may include identity and contact data, account references, business data, financial or transaction data, document and template content, images, signatures, technical identifiers, and any sensitive data Customer chooses to submit. Customer must not submit data prohibited by the contract or unsupported by the selected security and deployment configuration.

## 3. Documented instructions

PXA processes Customer Content only on documented instructions contained in the contract, Customer's use and configuration of the service, support requests, and additional lawful written instructions accepted by PXA. If PXA believes an instruction violates Data Protection Law, it will inform Customer unless prohibited by law and may suspend the affected processing.

PXA may process data where required by binding law. It will inform Customer before doing so unless the law prohibits notice for important public-interest reasons.

## 4. Confidentiality and personnel

PXA limits access to authorized personnel and contractors who need it to operate or support the service. They are bound by confidentiality obligations and receive appropriate security and privacy instruction.

## 5. Security

PXA maintains measures appropriate to risk, including as applicable:

- tenant isolation and organization-scoped authorization;
- least-privilege roles and protected administrator operations;
- host-only HttpOnly session cookies, anti-forgery protection, and credential safeguards;
- encryption in transit and deployment-appropriate encryption at rest;
- secrets management and no secret values in application logs;
- minimized operational logs, metrics, traces, and auditable privileged actions;
- vulnerability, dependency, build, backup, recovery, availability, and incident controls;
- bounded retention and object deletion for document jobs; and
- development, test, and production separation.

Customer is responsible for its users, access configuration, endpoint security, lawful content, selected region, integrations, exports, and any On-Premise infrastructure under its control. Detailed technical and organizational measures applicable to the ordered deployment are provided in the security schedule or order.

## 6. Subprocessors

Customer gives general authorization for PXA to use subprocessors needed to provide the service. PXA will maintain a current list identifying the subprocessor, service, and processing location before production use. PXA imposes materially equivalent data-protection obligations and remains responsible for subprocessor performance as required by law.

PXA will give reasonable advance notice of a new subprocessor. Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within the stated notice period. The parties will seek a reasonable alternative; if none is available, either party may terminate only the affected service, with a pro-rata refund of unused prepaid fees where appropriate.

Customer-configured providers in an On-Premise or customer-controlled deployment are Customer's responsibility unless the order states otherwise.

## 7. International transfers

PXA will not transfer protected personal data to a country lacking legally recognized adequate protection without a lawful safeguard. Depending on the origin and destination, safeguards may include the Swiss or EU standard contractual clauses, applicable modules and Swiss adaptations, the UK Addendum, approved certification, or another valid mechanism. PXA will provide relevant transfer information and implement supplementary measures where required.

## 8. Data-subject requests

Taking into account the nature of processing, PXA provides reasonable technical and organizational assistance for Customer to respond to requests for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability. If PXA receives a request concerning Customer Content, it will direct the requester to Customer and notify Customer where lawful. Additional work beyond standard product controls may be chargeable if permitted by law and the contract.

## 9. Security incidents

PXA will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed personal-data breach affecting Customer Content. The notice will include available information about the nature, likely consequences, affected data, measures taken, and a contact point. PXA may provide information in phases and will reasonably assist Customer's investigation and notification duties. Notification is not an admission of fault.

Customer must promptly notify PXA of compromised credentials, unlawful instructions, or incidents in Customer-controlled systems that affect the service.

## 10. Compliance assistance

PXA provides information reasonably needed for Customer's data-protection impact assessments, prior consultations, processing records, and security reviews, taking into account the nature of processing and information available to PXA.

## 11. Audit

PXA makes available relevant independent reports, certifications, security documentation, and questionnaire responses where available. If these are insufficient for a statutory audit need, Customer may request one audit per year on reasonable notice, during business hours, under confidentiality, without exposing another customer's data or compromising security. Additional or repeated audits are at Customer's reasonable cost unless an audit identifies a material breach by PXA. A regulator's lawful powers are unaffected.

## 12. Return and deletion

During the contract and any stated export period, Customer can retrieve supported Customer Content. On termination or Customer instruction, PXA deletes or returns Customer Content unless law requires retention. Synchronous content is transient. Transient queued content is deleted after successful download or within 24 hours after completion; explicitly retained content is kept for up to seven days; minimized terminal metadata is kept for 30 days. Other workspace content follows the contracted retention and closure schedule.

Deletion from active systems may be followed by expiry from encrypted backups under a bounded rotation schedule. Data isolated in a backup is not restored except for recovery and remains protected and unavailable for ordinary processing. A documented legal hold may suspend deletion only for the affected data and duration.

## 13. Customer obligations

Customer will provide lawful instructions, required notices and consents, an appropriate legal basis, and a contact for privacy matters. Customer will use data minimization, avoid unsupported categories, configure appropriate access and retention, and independently verify that PXA's service, region, and safeguards meet Customer's regulatory duties.

## 14. Liability and order of precedence

Liability under this DPA is subject to the contract's valid liability provisions, without limiting rights or liabilities that cannot be limited under Data Protection Law. For a conflict concerning processing of Customer Content, this DPA prevails over the Terms; a signed, more protective data-processing term prevails over this DPA.
