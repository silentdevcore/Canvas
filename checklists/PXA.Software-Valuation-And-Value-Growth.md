# PXA Software Valuation and Value Growth

## Purpose

Preserve the current indicative valuation of Power Dox Automation (PXA), the
assumptions behind it, and the development and commercial milestones that could
increase its value.

Assessment date: 2026-08-24.

This document is a strategic planning estimate, not an independent valuation,
fairness opinion, tax assessment, investment recommendation, or guaranteed sale
price. It must be updated with verified revenue, cost, retention, customer,
legal, security, and market data before any financing or sale process.

## Current Repository Baseline

The repository assessment used the following approximate tracked scope:

- 223,000 source-code lines after excluding common generated and build output.
- 106 .NET projects.
- 51 .NET test projects.
- Approximately 1,400 relevant tracked source and documentation files.
- Designer, PDF engine, importers, migration providers, Viewer, Admin, Account,
  subscriptions, licensing, observability, and Beta MCP foundations.

Lines of code and project count are not direct measures of commercial value.
They help estimate replacement effort, while buyers primarily value verified
customer demand, recurring revenue, profitability, retention, defensible IP,
operational quality, and manageable risk.

## Current Indicative Valuation

Assumption: PXA has little or no verified recurring revenue and is offered as a
software/IP asset rather than as a proven SaaS company.

| Transaction context | Indicative value |
| --- | ---: |
| Source-code and IP asset sale | CHF 250,000-750,000 |
| Prepared asset deal including documentation, brand, domains, and deployment material | CHF 500,000-1,000,000 |
| Strategic acquirer with a specific need for PXA migration technology | CHF 800,000-1,500,000 |
| Indicative initial asking range | CHF 700,000-900,000 |
| Indicative likely closing range without meaningful ARR | CHF 450,000-750,000 |

The estimated professional replacement cost is approximately CHF 1.5-4 million,
depending on team composition, implementation quality, required fidelity, and
delivery time. Replacement cost is not the same as market value. A buyer will
normally apply a substantial discount when revenue, independent validation,
customer references, and production operations are absent.

## Development-State Scenarios

These ranges assume no large recurring-revenue base. They are directional and
overlap because execution quality and buyer fit matter more than feature count.

| Product state | Indicative value without substantial ARR |
| --- | ---: |
| Current repository state | CHF 250,000-750,000 |
| P0 gaps closed and a stable release delivered | CHF 700,000-1,500,000 |
| Enterprise-ready deployment, public SDKs, and repeatable quality evidence | CHF 1.2-2.5 million |
| Mature platform with initial reference customers and strategic buyer interest | CHF 2-4 million |

Feature investments are not additive line items. Completing several milestones
may reduce the same underlying technical or commercial risk and therefore cannot
be counted twice.

## Highest-Value Development Milestones

### PDF Security and Standards

Indicative technical value effect: 20-40 percent.

- Digital signatures and validation.
- Modern AES encryption.
- PDF/A generation and validation.
- PDF/UA and Tagged PDF accessibility.
- Permanent, secure redaction.
- Compliance diagnostics and repair workflows.

These capabilities are important for regulated and Enterprise buyers.

### Existing-PDF Editing and Viewer Maturity

Indicative technical value effect: 15-30 percent.

- Reliable preservation of complex PDF object graphs.
- Page and content editing with safe save behavior.
- Advanced forms and annotations.
- Progressive rendering for large documents.
- Comparison, signature validation, and form design.
- Repeatable browser, print, and export fidelity.

### Office Fidelity

Indicative technical value effect: 15-35 percent.

- Higher-fidelity Word layout and conversion.
- Spreadsheet formula calculation.
- Pivot Tables and native Spreadsheet charts.
- Deeper PowerPoint import, authoring, and export.
- Large real-world compatibility fixture collections.

### Enterprise Deployment

Indicative technical value effect: 15-30 percent.

- Supported Docker and On-Premise packages.
- Signed offline licensing.
- Upgrade, backup, restore, and disaster-recovery procedures.
- SSO, tenant isolation, audit, and operational hardening.
- Defined support and SLA processes.

### Public SDK and Documentation Ecosystem

Indicative technical value effect: 10-25 percent.

- Java, Python, TypeScript/JavaScript, and C# REST SDKs.
- Stable API and OpenAPI contracts.
- Package-registry delivery and version compatibility.
- Complete examples, tutorials, API reference, and migration guides.

### Independent Quality Evidence

Quality evidence may not add a visible feature, but it can materially reduce the
buyer's risk discount.

- Performance and memory benchmarks.
- Golden-document and visual-parity suites.
- Security assessment and dependency/license review.
- Compatibility matrices and real-world test corpora.
- Availability, backup, restore, and upgrade evidence.
- Customer references and documented support outcomes.

## Revenue-Based Valuation Scenarios

A practical planning range for a small private SaaS business is approximately
3-5 times annual recurring revenue (ARR). Higher multiples require evidence such
as strong growth, low churn, high gross margin, efficient operations, durable
Enterprise contracts, and defensible differentiation. Lower growth, customer
concentration, weak profitability, or material technical risk can produce a
lower multiple.

| Verified ARR | Indicative enterprise value |
| ---: | ---: |
| CHF 100,000 | CHF 300,000-500,000 |
| CHF 250,000 | CHF 750,000-1.5 million |
| CHF 500,000 | CHF 1.5-3 million |
| CHF 1 million | CHF 3-6 million |
| CHF 2 million | CHF 6-12 million |

At CHF 1 million ARR with healthy growth, retention, margin, and Enterprise
quality, an indicative CHF 4-6 million range would be a reasonable central
planning case.

Market references:

- SaaS Capital estimated a 4.8x private SaaS multiple for bootstrapped companies
  and 5.3x for equity-backed companies in its 2025 model:
  https://www.saas-capital.com/blog-posts/private-saas-company-valuations-multiples/
- Acquire.com's January 2026 acquisition report notes that buyers commonly
  anchor smaller SaaS acquisitions on profit unless scale, growth, and retention
  support a revenue-based premium:
  https://blog.acquire.com/acquire-com-biannual-acquisition-multiples-report-jan-2026/

These market references are directional. PXA does not automatically qualify for
the referenced multiples.

## Most Effective Value-Growth Sequence

1. Close the highest-priority Enterprise security, standards, and reliability
   gaps.
2. Package one stable supported release with reproducible deployment and tests.
3. Win three to five paid pilot customers.
4. Sell migration as a separately priced professional service and product
   capability.
5. Reach at least CHF 250,000 ARR with measured retention and gross margin.
6. Add customer evidence, security review, operational metrics, and support
   processes.
7. Consider a strategic sale, investment, or broader financing process only
   after market evidence materially reduces buyer uncertainty.

Technical completion without customers might increase the current asset value
by approximately 50-150 percent. Technical completion plus reference customers,
recurring revenue, and healthy retention can increase it by several multiples.

## Due-Diligence Requirements

Before presenting a sale valuation, verify:

- [ ] Ownership and assignment of all source code, designs, domains, and marks.
- [ ] Third-party and open-source license compatibility, including distribution
  and commercial-use obligations.
- [ ] Reproducible builds from a clean environment.
- [ ] Test, security, performance, backup, and recovery evidence.
- [ ] Customer contracts, ARR, churn, retention, pipeline, and concentration.
- [ ] Cloud, support, payroll, contractor, and infrastructure costs.
- [ ] Data-protection, security, tax, and worldwide-sales readiness.
- [ ] Patent, trademark, naming, and infringement risks.
- [ ] All implemented, Beta, planned, and unsupported features are represented
  accurately.
- [ ] An independent Swiss valuation, tax, legal, and M&A review is obtained for
  an actual transaction.
